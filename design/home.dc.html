About Knights

Built for the road.
Trusted for the tour.

Knights Coaches runs a fleet of over 20 custom-converted Prevost H3-45 and X3-45 coaches, each built for overnight touring — full galleys, private rear lounges, onboard showers, and 6 to 14 bunks.

Every driver holds a CDL Class A or B license with a minimum of three years on entertainer coaches. As an EMC member with a satisfactory FMCSA rating, we're the crew that musicians, tour managers and production companies rely on coast to coast.
